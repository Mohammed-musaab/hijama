Prophet Muhammad (ﷺ) recommended performing Hijama on the 17th, 19th, and 21st nights of the Islamic lunar month. Modern biophysics reveals that during this waning lunar phase, bodily humors and toxins settle toward the surface of the body, making extraction maximally effective.

Pre-Hijama Preparation:

Fast or abstain from heavy meals for 2–3 hours prior. Stay well hydrated with plain water or honey water. Take a warm shower before arriving.

Post-Hijama Care:

Avoid milk, red meat, and heavy dairy for 24 hours. Drink warm fluids (dates/honey water). Rest and avoid strenuous workouts for 1–2 days.

Pre & Post Hijama Protocol

Essential instructions for a safe and effective cupping session

Before Hijama (Pre-Care):

  • Refrain from heavy food 2 to 3 hours prior to cupping.
  • Drink plenty of water or pure honey water.
  • Take a warm bath or shower before attending.
  • Inform the practitioner if you are on blood thinners.

After Hijama (Post-Care):

  • Drink lukewarm water or warm honey tea.
  • Avoid red meat, milk, and rich dairy products for 24 hours.
  • Do not take a cold shower for 12 hours after wet cupping.
  • Rest and avoid vigorous gym workouts for 24 hours.
