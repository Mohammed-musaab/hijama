The Prophetic Sunnah Hijama Calendar

Prophet Muhammad (ﷺ) recommended performing Hijama on the 17th, 19th, and 21st nights of the Islamic lunar month. Modern biophysics reveals that during this waning lunar phase, bodily humors and toxins settle toward the surface of the body, making extraction maximally effective.

Pre-Hijama Preparation:

Fast or abstain from heavy meals for 2–3 hours prior. Stay well hydrated with plain water or honey water. Take a warm shower before arriving.

Post-Hijama Care:

Avoid milk, red meat, and heavy dairy for 24 hours. Drink warm fluids (dates/honey water). Rest and avoid strenuous physical workouts for 1–2 days.

Pre & Post Hijama Protocol

Essential instructions for a safe and effective cupping session

Before Hijama (Pre-Care):

  • Refrain from heavy food 2 to 3 hours prior to cupping (an empty stomach promotes better detoxification).
  • Drink plenty of water or water with pure honey.
  • Take a warm bath or shower before attending to relax peripheral vessels.
  • Inform the practitioner if you are pregnant, anemic, or on blood-thinning medication.

After Hijama (Post-Care):

  • Drink lukewarm water or warm honey tea to restore vital energy.
  • Avoid red meat, milk, and rich dairy products for 24 hours to prevent digestive strain.
  • Do not take a cold shower for 12 hours after wet cupping; keep points clean and moisturized with olive or black seed oil.
  • Rest and avoid vigorous gym workouts or intense cardio for 24 hours.
