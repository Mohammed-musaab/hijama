Health Benefits

The suction acts like a mini vacuum on your skin, pulling trapped or slow-moving blood to the surface. This causes your blood vessels to open wide, bringing a fresh wave of oxygen and nutrients straight to tired, tight muscles.
Your lymphatic system is your body’s drainage network, but it doesn't have a pump like your heart to keep things moving. The suction lifts the tissue, unblocking trapped fluids and flushing away leftover cellular waste and acid build-up that make you feel sore.
Cupping stimulates nerve endings in your skin to send fast signals to your spinal cord, which effectively "blocks" your brain from registering pain (like rubbing a bumped elbow). At the same time, your brain releases its own natural painkillers—endorphins—to help you feel relaxed and pain-free.
The gentle pull creates tiny, harmless micro-signals that trick your body into repair mode. It tells your immune system to stop sending "irritation" chemicals and start producing healing proteins, turning off long-term, dull acheiness and speeding up recovery.

Pre & Post Hijama Protocol

Essential instructions for a safe and effective cupping session

Before Hijama (Pre-Care):

  • Have a meal prior to cupping.
  • Drink plenty of water.
  • Inform the practitioner if you are on blood thinners.

After Hijama (Post-Care):

  • Drink plenty of water.
  • Avoid Chicken, red meat, milk, and rich dairy products for 1/2 hour.
  • Rest and avoid vigorous gym workouts for 24 hours.
