Authentic Hadiths on the Blessings of Hijama

Hijama (cupping) is a sacred Sunnah explicitly instructed by the angels during the miraculous Night Journey (Isra' & Mi'raj).

Sahih al-Bukhari 5697

«إِنَّ أَفْضَلَ مَا تَدَاوَيْتُمْ بِهِ الْحِجَامَةُ»

The Messenger of Allah (ﷺ) said: "Indeed, the best of remedies you have is Hijama (cupping)..."

Scholarly Insight: Highlights cupping as an unmatched preventive and restorative medical practice.
Sunan Ibn Majah 3477 (Sahih)

«مَا مَرَرْتُ لَيْلَةَ أُسْرِيَ بِي بِمَلإٍ مِنَ الْمَلاَئِكَةِ إِلاَّ قَالُوا يَا مُحَمَّدُ مُرْ أُمَّتَكَ بِالْحِجَامَةِ»

The Prophet (ﷺ) said: "During my Night Journey, I did not pass by any group of angels except that they said: 'O Muhammad, order your Ummah with Hijama.'"

Scholarly Insight: Demonstrates the divine status of cupping therapy delivered directly by the angels.
Sahih al-Bukhari 5680

«الشِّفَاءُ فِي ثَلاَثَةٍ: شَرْبَةِ عَسَلٍ، وَشَرْطَةِ مِحْجَمٍ، وَكَيَّةِ نَارٍ، وَأَنْهَى أُمَّتِي عَنِ الْكَيِّ»

The Prophet (ﷺ) said: "Healing is in three things: A drink of honey, the incision of a cupper, and cauterizing with fire..."

Scholarly Insight: The incision of the cupper releases congested, impure capillary blood and cellular waste.
Sunan Abi Dawud 3861

«مَنِ احْتَجَمَ لِسَبْعَ عَشْرَةَ وَتِسْعَ عَشْرَةَ وَإِحْدَى وَعِشْرِينَ كَانَ شِفَاءً مِنْ كُلِّ دَاءٍ»
